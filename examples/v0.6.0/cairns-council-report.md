# 8. Evacuation Planning
The evacuation planning process involves:
- **Step 1 - Research**: Check with the local council and library for information on potential hazards, local warning systems, evacuation process, and nominated evacuation routes. Identify support agencies and contact details.
- **Step 2 - Record Details**: Identify a safe place to evacuate to, such as family or friends living in secure accommodation further inland on higher ground. Record contact and location details. Create a checklist for everything needed before evacuating.
- **Step 3 - Prepare Your Evacuation Kit**: Gather essential supplies, including first aid, food, water, and emergency documents. Keep the kit in a sturdy, easy-to-carry bag or waterproof storage box.

# 9. Candidate Assembly Point Criteria
Criteria for candidate assembly points include:
- Secure and accessible location
- Adequate shelter and facilities
- Proximity to essential services
- Clear signage and communication
- Adequate space for assembly and movement

# 11. Communication and Inclusion Needs
- **Internal Communication**: Regular updates and drills to ensure staff and volunteers are prepared.
- **Public/Parent Communication**: Clear and accessible information through multiple channels, including social media, local media, and community meetings.
- **Multilingual Needs**: Provide plain-language and multilingual communication for warnings, drills, and parent/community updates.
- **Backup Channels**: Ensure multiple communication channels are available, including text messages, social media, and local radio.

# 12. First Aid, Training and Exercises
- **First Aid**: Provide first aid training for staff and volunteers.
- **Smoke/Heat Exposure**: Develop a plan to support people with health vulnerabilities during smoke and heat exposure.
- **AED/Burn Response**: Ensure access to Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) and burn response equipment.
- **Drill Frequency**: Conduct regular evacuation drills and first aid training exercises.
- **Exercise Records**: Maintain records of all drills and exercises for review and improvement.
